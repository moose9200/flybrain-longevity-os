# FlyBrain Longevity OS — Product One-Pager

## Problem

Supplement and nutraceutical teams bet on ingredients with no fast, transparent healthspan signal. Mammalian trials are slow and costly; marketing claims outrun biology. Teams need a fail-fast screen before committing formulation, testing and inventory spend.

## Method

Software-only Drosophila healthspan screener. Zero wet lab, zero budget per run. We rank 50 compounds on median lifespan change plus climbing/flight and stress resistance, graded A (fetched) / B / C (validate). Top hits map to 6 brain circuits (5 unisex + 1 dimorphic excluded by default) for mechanistic context. Every result carries strain, dose, sex and source DOI.

## Data sources

- Janelia Male CNS v1.0 (CC-BY, ~166k neurons) — primary circuit maps; cite Berg et al. Cell 2026.
- FlyWire Female FAFB v783 (~139k neurons) — female complement; cite Dorkenwald et al. Nature 2024.
- Interactive: neuPrint (male-cns:v1.0); bulk: gs://flyem-male-cns/v1.0/; female: https://codex.flywire.ai/
